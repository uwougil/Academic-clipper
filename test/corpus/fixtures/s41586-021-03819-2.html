Abstract

Proteins are essential to life. Here we provide the computational method AlphaFold, which predicts three-dimensional protein structures with atomic accuracy across the proteome even in cases where no homologous structure is known.

Main

AlphaFold integrates evolutionary and physical constraints into an end-to-end differentiable neural network 1,2. The architecture overview is shown in Figure 1.

Accuracy is benchmarked on CASP14 targets in Figure 2. Predictions routinely achieve median \(\mathrm{C}_\alpha\) backbone error with \(\mathrm{RMSD} < 1.0\ \text{Å}\), high global distance test score \(\mathrm{GDT\_TS} \ge 90\), and residue confidence \(\mathrm{pLDDT} > 70\), quantified via distance error metric:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{FAPE}} = \frac{1}{N_{\mathrm{frames}}} \sum_{i,j} \min\left( d(T_i^{-1} \mathbf{x}_j, T_i^{*-1} \mathbf{x}_j^*), d_{\mathrm{clamp}} \right)$$
