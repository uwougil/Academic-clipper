Abstract

Novel functional materials enable clean-energy transitions. Using Graph Networks for Materials Exploration (GNoME), we expand the number of known stable materials by an order of magnitude, predicting 2.2 million crystal structures.

Main

Computational discovery operates via active learning on convex hull energy calculations 1,2. The discovery loop is schematized in Figure 1.

Thermodynamic stability is evaluated relative to competing phases via energy distance to the convex hull:

$$\Delta E_{\mathrm{hull}}(\mathbf{x}) = E(\mathbf{x}) - \sum_i c_i \mu_i$$
(1)

We discover thousands of layered oxides including derivatives of \(\mathrm{Li}_x\mathrm{FePO}_4\), perovskites like \(\mathrm{BaTiO}_3\), and ternary chalcogenides as evaluated in Table 1 and Figure 2.

Electrochemical properties are summarized in Table 2, while full crystal coordinate tables are provided in external supplementary data Table 3. Experimental synthesis validations are depicted in Figure 3 and Extended Data Fig. 1 through Extended Data Fig. 3.

Methods

DFT relaxations were conducted using the VASP package with PAW pseudopotentials and PBEsol functional.
