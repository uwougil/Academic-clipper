Abstract

The human reference genome has served as the backbone of modern genomics. Here the Human Pangenome Reference Consortium presents a draft human pangenome reference created from 47 phased, diploid assemblies from a genetically diverse cohort.

Main

Standard linear reference genomes suffer from reference bias 1,2. Our graph-based pangenome captures diverse structural variants with \(k\)-mer size \(k = 31\), consensus quality \(\mathrm{QV} \ge 50\), and contig continuity \(N_{50} > 50\ \mathrm{Mb}\) as shown in Figure 1.

Haplotype phasing and error rates across ancestries are evaluated in Figure 2 and Table 1.

Novel euchromatic sequence additions and satellite DNA resolution are shown in Figure 3, Figure 4, and Table 2, with supplementary validation in Extended Data Fig. 1 through Extended Data Fig. 4.

Methods

Samples were selected in accordance with ethical standards from the 1000 Genomes Project cohort.
