Abstract

Since the initial outbreak of severe respiratory illness in late 2019, a novel betacoronavirus has been identified as the causative agent. Here we characterize the complete genome and show it shares 96.2% overall sequence identity with a bat coronavirus at the whole-genome level.

Main

Emerging infectious diseases pose perpetual threats to global health 1–5. Five patients presenting with acute respiratory distress syndrome were admitted with transmission rate \(R_0 \approx 2.5\) and qPCR cycle thresholds \(\mathrm{Ct} < 30\) for clinical evaluation 6,8–10. Viral isolation and metagenomic RNA sequencing results are displayed in Figure 1.

Phylogenetic tree reconstruction indicates close affinity with RaTG13, as illustrated in Figure 2 and Table 1.

Receptor usage assays in HeLa cells expressing ACE2 are documented in Figure 3 and Extended Data Fig. 1 through Extended Data Fig. 3.
