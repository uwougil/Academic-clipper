Abstract

The picrotoxane sesquiterpenoids possess densely functionalized, highly oxygenated structures that antagonize ionotropic GABA and glycine receptors. Here we report the total synthesis of 25 picrotoxane natural products through virtual library selection and convergent modular assembly.

Main

The picrotoxanes feature intricate carbobicyclic scaffolds with multiple contiguous stereocentres 1,2. The strategic disconnection commences from enone 1 as shown in Figure 1.

Subjecting intermediate 2a to Lewis-acid catalysis at \(-78\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}\) provided the trans-fused bicyclic core 3 with optical rotation \({[\alpha]}_{\mathrm{D}}^{20} = +45.2\) in 82% isolated yield and enantiomeric excess \(\mathrm{ee} > 99\%\), establishing the (5R,8S,10R) configuration as illustrated in Figure 2.

Completion of picrotoxinin 4 was achieved as documented in Figure 3 and Table 1, with single-crystal X-ray verification in Extended Data Fig. 1 and spectroscopic comparison in Extended Data Fig. 2.

Methods

All air- and moisture-sensitive manipulations were carried out in flame-dried glassware under an argon atmosphere.
